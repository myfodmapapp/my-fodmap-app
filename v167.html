Your personal records

Your results are personal observations, not a medical diagnosis.

🔄 How My FODMAP learns

Every time you record a food or scanned product, the app keeps the date, portion, symptoms and product ingredients. It uses those records to personalise future checks.

📚 My Product Library

Products you have scanned or taught My FODMAP are stored locally. You can correct the name, ingredients or serving size at any time — no new app version is needed for your own corrections.
